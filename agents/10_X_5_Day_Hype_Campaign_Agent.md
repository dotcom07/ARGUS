
## Claim Boundary

Never overclaim.

Argus can verify:

```text
capture path/provenance from an Argus-controlled native Android capture flow
manifest commitment generated by the Rust proof core
required device-side evidence committed into the proof
production commitments registered with sponsored gas by an authorized production relayer fee payer under the production-pinned known Argus Registry program ID and trusted registry configuration
```

Onchain role to emphasize:

```text
Solana is the public tamper-evidence layer for accepted Argus proof commitments.
```

Do not imply that Solana directly inspects Android camera evidence or that Argus is backend-free. Photos and evidence bundles stay offchain; the verifier recomputes the offchain bundle against the onchain commitment record.

Argus does not prove:

```text
the physical scene is true
the item belongs to the seller
the photographer did not stage the scene
the photographer did not photograph a screen
all AI-generated media is detected
the physical camera sensor cryptographically signed the pixels
```

Preferred wording:

```text
Argus verifies the required capture path, not scene truth.
```

## Two Optional Threads

### Thread 1 - Why AI Detection Is Not Enough

```text
1. AI-generated media is improving fast.
2. After-upload detection creates an endless classifier race.
3. Evidence photos need provenance at capture time.
4. Argus verifies that a photo came from an embedded native capture flow.
5. The proof commitments are recorded externally so verification does not depend only on file-bound metadata.
6. Marketplace is one demo; the same primitive expands to insurance, returns, rentals, and field evidence.
```

### Thread 2 - Argus Architecture in 6 Steps

```text
1. Partner app calls the Argus RN SDK.
2. Kotlin opens a native Android camera flow.
3. Device-side evidence is summarized and committed.
4. Rust builds a deterministic manifest and proof ID.
5. An authorized production relayer validates the proof bundle and sponsors registration under the production-pinned known Argus Registry program ID.
6. The verifier shows proof status, trusted registry configuration, authorized production relayer, and limitations.
```

```text
Argus gives platforms a provenance signal for user-submitted photo evidence.
```
