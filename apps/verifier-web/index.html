Proof boundary

Production Argus verifies

  • Photo bytes pass the structural JPEG-like byte gate and match the manifest image commitment.
  • Canonical manifest hash matches a production-pinned Argus Registry commitment.
  • Active registry record matches trusted registry configuration, authorized production relayer fee payer, sponsored gas, and fee-payer binding.
  • Partner/use-case/app identity policy, committed device-evidence policy, and active status are acceptable for capture-provenance verification.
  • Level 3 requires validated Keystore public key/signature evidence. Level 4 requires trusted-root hardware attestation; otherwise show Level 3/2 fallback.
  • Device-side evidence was committed without raw image storage on-chain.

Argus does not verify

  • Physical scene truth.
  • Ownership, condition, or legal validity of the submitted subject.
  • Image forensics or AI detection for images submitted outside Argus capture.
  • Whether the camera photographed a screen.
  • Complete prevention of rooted, emulated, or mock-camera environments.
